Measure voltage with a multimeter

Voltage is the push that moves charges through a circuit. A multimeter can measure it. You will learn how to set up the meter, what “across the load” means, and then measure parts of a real circuit.

When you finish, you can:

  • Set up a multimeter to measure voltage.
  • Measure across a battery, a load and a wire.
  • Explain why the meter goes beside a part, not inside the loop.

Have a real multimeter? Follow along with it. Use the virtual one on these slides to practice. Readings are simulated so every class sees the same numbers.

1. Meet the multimeter

You used a multimeter to measure resistance. Today you will use the same tool to measure voltage. Only one thing is very different: for voltage, the circuit has power.

Tap a part name. The part lights up on the meter.

2. What does “across the load” mean?

Voltage is always a difference between two points, like the height at the top and the bottom of a slide. So you never touch just one spot. You put the two probes on two sides of something.

Load

The part that uses the energy. A light bulb, motor, buzzer or speaker is a load. In this circuit, the load is the light bulb.

Across

One probe on each side of a part. Across the load means the red probe on one side of the bulb and the black probe on the other side.

The meter goes beside the part

✔ Right: beside the bulb
The loop is not broken. The bulb stays on. This is called hooking it up in parallel.
✘ Wrong: inside the loop
Do not cut the loop and put the meter in the line. The bulb goes out.

3. Set up the meter and measure a battery

Safety rule

Voltage is measured with the power on. Always check the dial is on V before you touch the probes to a circuit.

  1. 1Drag the black plug into the COM port.
  2. 2Drag the red plug into the VΩ port.
  3. 3Turn the dial to V. (Tap the V on the meter.)
  4. 4Drag the red probe tip to the + end of the battery (point A).
  5. 5Drag the black probe tip to the − end of the battery (point D).
  6. 6Read the number and units: V

4. Measure across parts of a circuit

Set the dial to V. Drag each probe tip to a point (A, B, C or D), or pick a probe and tap a point. Do each mission. Tap Save this reading after each one.

  1. 1Switch closed. Measure across the battery (A and D).
  2. 2Switch closed. Measure across the load (B and C).
  3. 3Measure across a wire (C and D).
  4. 4Open the switch. Measure across the load again.
  5. 5Switch still open. Measure across the switch (A and B).
  6. 6Use 3 cells. Measure across the battery.
