    Put a lesson into Canvas

    1. In your Canvas course, create a page (or edit one).
    2. In the editor, click the </> HTML editor button at the bottom right.
    3. On this hub, click Copy Canvas HTML on the lesson.
    4. Paste into the HTML editor. Click </> again to see the page. Click Save.
    5. Run Canvas's Accessibility Checker. The pages are written to pass it (alt text under 120 characters, table captions and header scopes).

    Times are for a full, independent lesson. For a 45-minute live class, pick the core parts and assign the rest. Give Quest 1 after 3.04, Quest 2 after 3.09, and the Unit 3 Mission after the 3.12 review.
